# Resturant Management System
## Overview
      The Restaurant Management System is a software application designed to manage the daily operations of a  restaurant efficiently. It provides a centralized platform for managing customer orders, menu items, billing, payments, inventory and deliveries. The system will support different users, including the owner, manager, cashier, waiter, order taker and delivery boy. Its main purpose is to reduce the work manually, minimize errors, save time, minimize discrepancies and improve customer satisfaction.
## Scope
 
- In Scope: Menu and order management.
- In Scope: Table management.
- In Scope: Billing and payment management.
- In Scope: Inventory management.
- In Scope: Staff management.
- In Scope: Delivery management.
- In Scope: Sales and inventory reports.
- Out Of Scope: Food preparation and cooking.
- Out Of Scope: Employee payroll.
- Out Of Scope: Multiple branch management.
- Out Of Scope: Supplier payments.

## Stakeholders

- Owner: Monitors business performance and revenue.
- Manager: Manages daily operations and staff.
- Waiter: Takes customer orders and manages tables.
- Cashier: Handles billing and payments.
- Kitchen Staff: Receives orders and updates order status.
- Delivery Person: Handles delivery orders.
- Project Maintainer: Maintains and updates the system.

## Functional Requirements

- FR-01: The system shall manage menu items.
- FR-02: The system shall record customer orders.
- FR-03: The system shall manage table availability.
- FR-04: The system shall send orders to the kitchen.
- FR-05: The system shall generate bills and record payments.
- FR-06: The system shall track inventory and low-stock alerts.
- FR-07: The system shall manage staff assignments and attendance.
- FR-08: The system shall manage delivery status.
- FR-09: The system shall generate sales and inventory reports.

## Nonfunctional Requirements

- NFR-01: The system shall display order lists within 2 seconds under agreed test conditions.
- NFR-02: The system shall provide secure login and role-based access.
- NFR-03: The system shall have a simple and user-friendly interface.
- NFR-04: The system shall maintain accurate records.

## Acceptance Criteria

- FR-01: Added menu items shall appear in the menu.
- FR-02: Saved orders shall appear in the order list.
- FR-03: Table availability shall display correctly.
- FR-04: Orders shall be visible to kitchen staff.
- FR-05: Bills shall show correct items and total amounts.
- FR-06: Low stock shall generate an alert.
- FR-07: Staff assignments shall be recorded.
- FR-08: Delivery status shall update correctly.
- FR-09: Sales reports shall show recorded transactions.

## Assumptions and Open Questions

## Assumptions:

- A-01: The restaurant will have compatible devices and internet access.
- A-02: Staff will have role-based accounts.

## Open Questions:

- Q-01: Which payment methods will be supported?
- Q-02: Will customer reservations be included?
- Q-03: Will inventory update automatically after orders?
